play lab — who owns the clock under load?

Both boxes run the same when: '.on', play: 1 journey through the REAL engine. The left one's keyframes are simple from→to, so emission hands the clock to a CSS transition (the compositor). The right one's are a three-stop pulse, which only the JS ramp can express. Toggle mid-flight (~60% is the interesting spot) to feel the reversal semantics; then drag the jank slider up and toggle again — the decider question is how much of each lane's character survives a busy main thread.

   (noticeably quicker home); the ramp reverses at constant speed.
   The decider question: is that asymmetry acceptable as the price of
   compositor-owned plays, or does it read as two materials?
