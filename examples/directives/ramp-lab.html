ramp lab — how should a reversed play retime?

One toggle drives four boxes through the same 1s journey (110px down). Toggle it back mid-flight — around 60% is the interesting spot — and judge the reverse. Then drag the jank slider up and toggle again: the JS lanes stutter with the main thread, the CSS lane does not.

A — today: reverse re-runs the FULL 1s regardless of where it turned. Turning at 60% crawls back at 0.6× speed.

B

B — proportional: reverse duration = distance × play. Turning at 60% takes 600ms — constant speed both ways.

C

C — settle: exponential chase toward the target (the portal's ~195ms figure). Snappy, ease-out-ish by nature.

CSS

Reference — transition-play: compositor-owned. Watch it stay smooth at max jank.

what to feel for

A's tail-drag vs B's symmetry vs C's snap; whether C feels "different-material" from the way in (in = 1s eased, out = fast settle — asymmetric on purpose); and under jank, how much the JS lanes' character survives. The engine will keep ONE of these semantics for every reversed play.
